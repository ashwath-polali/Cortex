## brain files
create these files locally — they are gitignored and stay on your machine:
- core.md        personal identity, location, goals
- academics.md   courses, GPA, test scores
- awards.md      competition log
- projects.md    active projects and deadlines
- strategy.md    long-term plans and narrative
- claude_code.md coding style rules and preferences
- context.md     tone, stressors, people, patterns
run app.py once and the files will be auto-created with placeholder content.
edit them to reflect your own life. they will never be pushed to git.
